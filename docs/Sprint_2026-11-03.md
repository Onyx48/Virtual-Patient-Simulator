# Sprint Update — Week of 2026-11-03

## Done this sprint

### History Taking: updated feedback questions

- The standard feedback questions now follow the client's full list: **24 standard questions + 5 case-specific = 29**.
- New questions 19 to 24 cover politeness, clear questions, tactful sensitive questions, the patient's beliefs and concerns, structure and summary, and explaining the symptoms.
- Each question is linked to one of the 8 History Taking criteria, as in the client's report template.
- After each session, every question is marked **Yes / Partly / No** with a short quote from the student as evidence.
- Educators and admins see this per question in the student's session view. Students do not see it yet.

### Self-reflection

A short spoken reflection (about 3 minutes) now takes place after History Taking, before the student sees any feedback.

- An AI guide asks up to six short, neutral questions, following the client's self-reflection guide. It never judges or gives hints.
- It ends with the student's self-rating (1 to 10) and a one-line summary, saved with the session.
- Educators see the self-rating, summary and status, plus a marker if the student seemed distressed.
- Japanese scenarios get the reflection in Japanese.
- Feedback is prepared during the reflection, so it is ready as soon as the reflection ends.

### Learning objectives on scenarios

- Each scenario now has **Learning Objectives** (2 to 4). The AI writes them when creating a scenario, and the educator can edit them on the form.
- They are used by the self-reflection guide.
